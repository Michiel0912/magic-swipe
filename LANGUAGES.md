# Languages

Magic Swipe follows the system language by default. On Android 13 and later, users can select a separate app language from Android's per-app language settings.

## Supported locales

| Region focus | Languages |
|---|---|
| Global / Europe | English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Romanian, Czech, Hungarian, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish |
| Latin America | Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese |
| Middle East & Africa | Arabic, Persian, Hebrew, Turkish, English, French, Swahili |
| South Asia | English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Urdu |
| South-Eastern Asia | Indonesian, Malay, Thai, Vietnamese, Filipino |
| Greater China | Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese |

## Why these languages?

The selection aims to cover HONOR's most important and fastest-growing international regions without limiting the app to HONOR devices. Latin America is a major overseas volume region for HONOR, the Middle East & Africa is a major growth region, Europe remains important for the brand, and South-Eastern Asia is expanding.

Translations can always be improved. Native-speaker corrections are welcome through pull requests or issues.
